Hello, I'm Sophia

I'm Sophia, I'm 17 years old, and I'm a Grade 12 student at UST Angelicum College. I'm the eldest in my family, with one brother who is a year younger than me. I dream of becoming an architect.

I grew up in Quezon City, sharing a house with my family and my aunt's family. In 2019 we moved to a new home, still in Quezon City.

I've also been interested in coding for a long time. My dad works in IT and taught me the basics, and we learned HTML in elementary, so I'm really grateful to have a foundation in coding. This site is my way of showing that technology can feel friendly.

my social change project

Carbon Muncher

A project that aims to help lessen air pollution by finding a way to reduce harmful gases released by vehicles.

The problem

A lot of vehicles release harmful gases into the air. This makes pollution worse and affects the health of people, especially those living in busy areas.

My idea

We want to create a device that can help absorb and convert harmful gases from vehicle emissions using technology.

Who it helps

People who live in areas with high pollution, especially:

  • Children
  • Elderly people
  • People with breathing problems

How you can help

  • Share our project with your friends and family.
  • Donate materials.
  • Help spread awareness about air pollution.
  • Volunteer to help us improve our idea. You can send ideas on the contact page.
